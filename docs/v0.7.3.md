## Convergence validation (post-release)

After the full benchmark, three review-layer changes targeted the five non-success cases: a transient-error retry for reviewer model calls, a wider but resolvable `delimiter_composition` cue, and evidence-gated domain-convention guidance in the reviewer prompt. A single real-model re-run of those five cases improved final success from 0/5 to 3/5 — the false reject and the `slugify` and `multifile_retry` false accepts were resolved, with `multifile_retry` exercising the full cue → forced-revision → guard-added → cue-cleared → accepted chain — and introduced no regressions on the 22 passing cases.

The two remaining false accepts (`multifile_proration`, `multifile_token_rotation`) are domain-semantic cases that require the holdout's exact contract (banker's rounding; raising on a cycle). The reviewer now engages `multifile_token_rotation` and forces a revision, but the candidate stops short of the precise raise; `multifile_proration` hinges on a rounding convention that is invisible without the holdout. This is the test-driven-repair information gap rather than a regression. See the [convergence validation record](results/v0.7.3-convergence-validation.md) for the per-case mechanism and honest attribution.
